### Added

- **Per-ticker Sortino ratio now also reports 10y/20y/30y windows** alongside
  the existing 1y value. The new `sortino_10y`/`sortino_20y`/`sortino_30y`
  fields are informational only — composite scores keep using the 1y
  `sortino_ratio` unchanged. A window reads `None` unless the ticker's price
  history reaches back far enough to cover it. Shown as new rows in the
  dashboard's detail panel.
