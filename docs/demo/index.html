Curve-steepness measure that smooths the short-end Fed-policy noise the more-famous 2y/10y carries. yfinance ships ^TNX (10y) and ^FVX (5y) as keyless Tier-0 tickers; ^IRX (3-month) drops in and out, so we stick to the pair that's always available.

Hand-curated regional / sector ticker lists. The weekly snapshot fetches fundamentals for every member; "empty" means the cron hasn't run yet.
federal-contractors
Orchestrator-driven: chains usaspending.gov → SEC EDGAR → yfinance to assemble the DoD Top-25 + curated seed. Rebuilt weekly.
aggregated-scores-best / -worst
Cross-universe ranking by mean of 7 composite scores (quality / dividend / growth / big_call / aaqs / hgi / screener_score). Top 25 + bottom 25 of the merged ticker pool. Hover the Score cell to see the row's mean — that's what put it in best or worst.
enhanced-kpi-screener-longs / -shorts
Conjunctive 14-criteria gate (Phase 2a of #192). A ticker is in longs iff it passes every long-side gate (market cap ≥ 2B, P/E ≤ 25, growth > 10%, beta > 1, ROE / ROA / ROIC > 10%, current / quick > 1, profit margin > 10%, buy rating, R&D > 5%) and in shorts iff it passes the inverted set. Counts vary — can legitimately be 0 each side when the source data is too benign or too sparse.
